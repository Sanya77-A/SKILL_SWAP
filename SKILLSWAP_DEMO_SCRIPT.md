# SkillSwap 3-5 Minute Demo Script

## Before the Demo

Run `SEED_DEMO_PASSWORD='<local-demo-password>' npm run seed` and keep that password outside source control. Fictional accounts include `samira@demo.skillswap.local` (learner), `arjun@demo.skillswap.local` (mentor), and `rhea@demo.skillswap.local` (admin). Confirm API health, frontend load, and the three sign-ins. Paid checkout is intentionally unavailable.

## Sequence

1. **Problem, 15 seconds**: “Learning marketplaces make people buy every session even when they could trade expertise. SkillSwap supports direct exchange, an internal credit ledger, and mentoring terms in one trusted workflow.”
2. **Landing, 15 seconds**: Point out the three exchange models and the honest paid-checkout boundary.
3. **Onboarding/profile, 20 seconds**: Show teach/learn skills, proficiency, availability, privacy, and professional evidence.
4. **Explore and matching, 25 seconds**: Search a canonical skill. Open a recommendation and explain that score/reasons are deterministic, not generated by AI.
5. **Proposal, 25 seconds**: Show offered/requested skills, sessions, duration, schedule, mode, and optional credits. Explain turn-based countering.
6. **Booking, 25 seconds**: Show local timezone, participant, duration, mode, credit terms, and confirmation. Mention the unique 15-minute participant slots.
7. **Messaging, 20 seconds**: Open the relationship-gated conversation, share a proposal/booking card, and point out durable receipts and retry-safe sends.
8. **SkillCredits, 20 seconds**: Show balance beside immutable ledger and the integrity indicator. Explain conditional debit and idempotent settlement.
9. **Learning, 25 seconds**: Show a roadmap, next milestone, progress, and mentor recommendations. Explain AI enrichment plus deterministic fallback.
10. **Mentor analytics, 15 seconds**: Switch to Arjun and show derived conversion/session/reputation metrics without claiming settled cash revenue.
11. **Admin moderation, 20 seconds**: Switch to Rhea and show capability-scoped reports, disputes, skill verification, role hierarchy, and audit trail.
12. **Architecture close, 20 seconds**: “React and Redux call an Express service layer; MongoDB owns state; Socket.IO delivers realtime events; AI is an optional grounded presentation layer. The hardest boundaries are booking, credits, auth rotation, block privacy, and replay safety.”

## Avoid During the Demo

Do not claim production users, revenue, internet-scale load, AI decision-making, real payments, distributed sockets, or formal WCAG certification. If a provider is not configured, demonstrate the grounded fallback as intentional behavior.
